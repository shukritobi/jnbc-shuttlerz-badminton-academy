Quick answers

Before your first session.

Who can join?

Badminton enthusiasts across age groups, with current coaching focused on beginners and intermediate players.

Where are sessions held?

Around Klang Valley. Contact the coach to check the closest currently available venue.

Are weekend slots available?

Yes, flexible weekday and weekend slots are advertised, subject to court and coach availability.

Can companies arrange training?

Yes, corporate and company team sessions are offered for fitness, engagement, teamwork and badminton development.

How do I check fees?

Rates depend on format, group size, venue and schedule. Send a WhatsApp enquiry for current options.
