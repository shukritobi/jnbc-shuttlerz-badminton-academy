The JNBC approach

Coaching beyond technique.

JNBC is built around communication and individual development. The coach first understands the student's needs, current ability and motivation, then builds correct fundamentals progressively rather than treating every player the same way.

The goal is not simply to repeat drills. Players learn how to move efficiently, understand the game, improve their confidence and apply what they learn during rallies, sparring and competition.

01 • UnderstandIdentify the student's ability, goals and learning needs.
02 • BuildEstablish good habits and sound fundamentals first.
03 • CommunicateUse clear feedback and immediate correction.
04 • ApplyTurn technique into practical match performance.

Player journey

Build the skill.
Then use it.

JNBC's training path links technical learning with practical application, including guided feedback, repetition, rallies, sparring and match situations.

1

Assess

Understand current movement, grip, timing, experience, fitness and goals.

2

Build

Develop correct grips, footwork, stroke mechanics and efficient movement.

3

Apply

Use the techniques through drills, rally patterns and structured sparring.

4

Progress

Improve consistency, tactical decisions, confidence, fitness and match readiness.

Quick answers

Before you join.

Who can join JNBC?

JNBC offers options for children, adults, recreational players, personal coaching, groups, competitive players, sparring and teams.

Does JNBC offer sparring?

Yes. Sparring Training is now included as an academy offer, allowing players to practise tactical decisions and match application under guided conditions.

Where are sessions held?

Across Klang Valley. Current court locations and session slots can be confirmed directly with the academy.

How do I register?

Use the player registration page, then the academy can confirm programme placement, timing and venue.

Can I pay online?

A dedicated fee-payment page is prepared. The live gateway can be connected once the academy's receiving account and provider are finalised.
